This procedure covers claims for defective goods bought in the shop {{shop_name}} from {{company_name}}, company ID {{company_id}}.

## Liability for defects

The seller is liable for defects present on delivery and for defects that appear within 24 months of delivery.

## How to make a claim

Email {{email}} or send the goods to {{returns_address}}. Include the order number, a description of the defect and the remedy you ask for. We will confirm receipt of your claim.

## Remedies

You may ask for repair or replacement; if that is not possible, a reasonable price reduction or withdrawal from the contract. We settle claims within 30 days.

## Costs

We reimburse reasonable costs of a justified claim.
