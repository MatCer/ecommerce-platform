# Cookies

The shop {{shop_name}} ({{company_name}}) uses cookies. Necessary cookies are stored without consent because the shop cannot work without them. Other cookies are used only with your consent, which you can change at any time in the cookie settings in the page footer.

## Necessary cookies

- **cart**: identifies your shopping cart (30 days).
- **__Host-cart**, **__Host-sid**: cart and sign-in on the checkout and account pages (up to 30 days).
- **consent**, **__Secure-consent_id**: your cookie choice (up to 13 months).

## Cookies with consent

- **Analytics**: traffic and site speed measurement.
- **Advertising**: ad effectiveness measurement.
- **Personalization**: for example recently viewed products.

Without consent we collect no identifiers; we only count anonymous page views in aggregate.
