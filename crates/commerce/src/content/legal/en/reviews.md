# Review verification
<!-- Platform template, not legal advice. Review with a lawyer before publishing. -->

The shop {{shop_name}} ensures that the product reviews it publishes come from customers who actually bought and received the product.

## Who can write a review

A review can only be written through a personal, single-use link that we create for a product of a delivered order. The link expires after 90 days. Reviews cannot be submitted any other way, so every published review is linked to a delivered order and is marked as a verified purchase.

## How we publish reviews

We check every review before publishing it. We publish positive and negative reviews alike and do not change their content. We reject only reviews that are unlawful, offensive, contain personal data of others, advertising or content unrelated to the product. We may add a public reply below a review.

We do not pay for reviews and do not offer any benefit in exchange for a positive one.

## Rating

The average rating of a product is calculated from all published reviews of that product. The name shown with a review is the one its author entered.
