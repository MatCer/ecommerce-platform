## Withdrawal instructions

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The period expires 14 days after the day on which you, or a third party other than the carrier indicated by you, acquire physical possession of the goods (for several goods delivered separately: of the last one).

To exercise the right, inform us by an unequivocal statement (for example an email to {{email}} or a letter to {{returns_address}}), or use the withdrawal form in the customer area of the shop. You may use the model form below, but it is not obligatory. The deadline is met if you send your statement before it expires.

## Effects of withdrawal

We will reimburse all payments received from you, including the costs of standard delivery, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal, using the same means of payment you used. We may withhold the reimbursement until we have received the goods back or you have supplied evidence of having sent them back.

Send the goods back to {{returns_address}} within 14 days of withdrawing. You bear the direct cost of returning the goods and are liable for any diminished value resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.
