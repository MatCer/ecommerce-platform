A · Ink + Forest. White header with ink text, warm muted panels, slate-blue identity (links, focus), deep forest green only on the money action with a white label (5.4:1). Closest to today's role model (blue identity + green buy) but calm.

Both palettes pass every documented pair (text ≥ 4.5:1, rating ≥ 3:1 on card, muted and review-wash; worst text pair: sale on sale-wash 5.0:1). All 23 token keys stay, including checkout's 8. Radius: Soft = sm 8 / md 12 (chips, inputs) / lg 16 (photos) / xl 24 (buy panel, drawer), buttons and badges fully round. Sharp = what the Shopify demos actually measure (median 0 on photos, cards, panels and inputs; 4px chips; pill buttons only on Concept and Impact). Card shadows go; one lift shadow stays for drawers and sheets.
